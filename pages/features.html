What you can do in Math Wizard

Math Wizard is built around one idea: you answer calculus problems to fight. Here is what the game and this website provide.

Android 12+ and 4 GB RAM Derivatives and integrals Story and PVP modes

3D visualization

Play in a 3D world that makes abstract calculus easier to picture. The visuals support the math. They do not replace working out the answer.

Solve to cast

During enemy encounters, the game presents a derivative or integral problem. Type your answer with the in-game algebra keypad. Pick the differentiation rule that applies: Constant, Variable, Power or Constant Multiple.

Instant checking

The Math Authority checks every answer. Correct answers restore or raise your health and energy. Wrong answers reduce them, so you get feedback on every problem.

Story mode

Play single-player campaign stages in order. Story mode works offline, so you can practice without a connection. Your progress is saved on the device and synced when you are back online.

PVP mode

Battle other players online. The Network Authority confirms each move so matches stay fair. PVP needs an internet connection over Wi-Fi or 4G LTE.

Spell locker

Unlock spells such as Fireball and drag them into your spell rings at Basic, Advanced and Ultimate levels. Use them in combat.

Progress tracking

Your profile shows level, daily streak, Calculus Power, accuracy rate, challenges won and play time. Progress is stored in the cloud, so it follows your account across devices.

Top-up

Buy in-game credits (shown as Aether) on the website. Pay with GCash or PayMaya through PayMongo, and choose a pack from 100 to 1,200 Aether.
