03. The Academy

For Engineering Students

Math Wizard is developed by students and educators at the National College of Science and Technology (NCST). This project transforms how engineering students learn integral and differential calculus.

Problem: Traditional calculus teaching relies on abstract concepts and textbook examples that don't engage modern learners.

Solution: We created an RPG where mathematical concepts become interactive challenges, making calculus tangible and enjoyable.

Target Audience: Engineering students struggling with calculus, visual learners, and anyone who believes math should be fun.
