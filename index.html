Core features

Game Mechanics

Built for students who learn better by doing: every mechanic ties math mastery to in-game progress.

Question-based progress

Students answer calculus problems to unlock the next stage and improve mastery.

Integral and differential focus

Content is centered on core engineering calculus topics with incremental difficulty.

Visual learning support

Graphical presentation helps learners understand abstract formulas and solution flow.

Gamified motivation

Rewards, stage goals, and progress feedback keep students engaged in practice.
