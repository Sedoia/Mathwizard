03. Project Overview

Designed For Modern Learners

Math Wizard is a 3D mobile educational platform focused on making advanced math concepts clearer, more engaging, and easier to practice.

Problem Addressed: Many learners find calculus abstract, difficult to visualize, and challenging to apply in practice.

Proposed Solution: Combine structured problem-solving, visual learning support, and interactive progression in one mobile experience.

Expected Outcome: Improved motivation, stronger conceptual understanding, and better long-term learning retention.
